Our story

Made by hand, every single morning.

Chef Giulia Ferraro learned to roll pasta at her grandmother's kitchen table outside Bologna. After twelve years cooking in New York and Rome, she opened Lume in 2019 with one rule: if it can be made in house, it is.

Every morning starts at the pasta bench. By the time the doors open, the dough has become tagliatelle, tonnarelli and stuffed tortelli, and the oven has been burning oak since noon.

  • Six fresh pastas, rolled and cut daily
  • Pizza and bread from a 900° oak-fired oven
  • 40+ wines from small Italian producers

“Candlelight, Italian records on the turntable, and the smell of the wood oven drifting through the room.”
